Chronological Heritage

Our Root Foundations

1990 LISAFI Era

Creation of the Libmanan Social Action Foundation Inc. (LISAFI) to anchor regional developmental programs.

1992 Prelature Socio-Pastoral Arm

Created into the Prelature of Libmanan Development Office (PLPDO) to serve directly as the Prelature’s dedicated socio-pastoral development operational arm.

1993 October 1
SEC Registration

PLDO was formally registered at the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), officially taking the name of Prelature of Libmanan Development Foundation Inc. (PLDFI)

2009 October 16
Modern Alignment

The Board unanimously approved the shifting of organizational nomenclature to Caritas Diocese of Libmanan, Inc. (CDL), cementing full structural integration with the global Caritas ecosystem network.
