Privacy Policy

Effective: September 29, 2026

Caritas Diocese of Libmanan, Inc. ("Caritas Libmanan", "we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules. This policy explains what this website (caritas-libmanan.org) does and does not collect.

1. Summary

This is an informational website. It has no public sign-up, no visitor accounts, no contact form, and no online payment form. Visitors can browse it without giving us any personal information.

2. Information we handle

  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically receives technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. We use this only to operate and secure the website.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to our email address, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the contents of your message.
  • Donations. Donations are made through your own bank or payment app using the QR code or bank details on our Donations page. This website does not collect card numbers, account numbers, or passwords. Your bank or payment provider processes the transaction and may share transaction details with us, such as the sender's name, amount, and reference number, which we use to acknowledge and record donations.
  • Staff accounts. A small number of authorized Caritas Libmanan staff have accounts, used only to publish program activity updates. We store only their email address and a securely hashed password; we never store passwords in readable form.
  • Published activity updates. Our "Recent Program Activities" section shows reports of our field work. These may include photographs of events and communities. If you appear in a photo and want it removed, please contact us.

3. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's session storage to remember the daily scripture verse. On a staff member's device, it also briefly stores a count of failed sign-in attempts to slow down password guessing. Neither contains personal information.

4. Third-party services

To display the website, your browser connects directly to these services, which receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel, which hosts the website.
  • Supabase, which stores program activity updates and staff sign-in.
  • Google Fonts and Tailwind CSS CDN, which provide the fonts and styling.
  • Facebook (Meta), whose page plugin on our Updates page shows our Facebook posts. Facebook may set its own cookies and receive information about your visit, especially if you are logged in to Facebook.
  • Images in activity updates may be loaded from external image hosts.

5. How we use information

We use the information above only to run and secure this website, reply to your messages, acknowledge donations, and report on our programs. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

6. Who can access it

Only authorized Caritas Libmanan personnel who need the information for their work can access it, along with the service providers listed above, to the extent needed to provide their services.

7. How long we keep it

We keep emails and donation records only as long as needed for the purpose they were given, or as required by law, such as accounting and tax rules. Hosting logs are kept by our providers according to their own retention periods. Staff account data is deleted when the staff member no longer needs access.

8. How we protect it

The website is served only over HTTPS. Only authorized staff can publish activity updates, and database access rules enforce this on the server. Staff passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider, and the site sends browser security headers.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal information is processed;
  • access your personal information;
  • object to processing;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information erased or blocked, where applicable;
  • data portability;
  • be compensated for damages; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, including removing a photo or deleting your information, email caritasdioceseoflibmanan@gmail.com or write to Caritas Diocese of Libmanan, Inc., Camarines Sur, Philippines.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website changes. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
